Near my hotel on Ambergris Caye

Ambergris Caye is long and thin, and getting around at night can take time. The finder splits it into three zones: San Pedro town, north of the bridge and south of town. Pick yours and the finder favors places in that zone, then adds the town at a lower score because it is a short ride away. We use zones rather than distances because we do not publish map pins until we can verify them.

How the matching works

Each restaurant in our directory carries editor’s tags taken from its sources: the type of food, the meals a source mentions and the occasion it suits (for example "where locals eat" for El Fogon, or "romantic" for a candlelit room described by a guidebook). Your choices add points; nothing is paid and no business is boosted. Meal tags come from sources and can change, so always check hours. Diet matches appear only where a source mentions vegetarian or vegan options; otherwise the finder says so and suggests dishes to ask for.

The evening plan suggests a drinks stop, dinner and a dessert or café in the same area when we have sourced options. Your selections are saved in the address bar so you can share them; nothing is sent to us.

Example searches

Staying south of San Pedro, date night: pick South of San Pedro, Fine dining and Romantic. Palmilla at Victoria House comes first, with Hidden Treasure in town as the short-ride alternative.

Caye Caulker breakfast: pick Caye Caulker, Breakfast and Breakfast. Errolyn’s and Ice & Beans lead.

Hopkins, authentic food: pick Hopkins, Garifuna and Where locals eat. Innie’s and Tugusina come up, with a reminder to order hudut ahead.

Cayo after a ruins tour: pick San Ignacio & Cayo, Belizean and Local. Cenaida’s and Pop’s appear, with Guava Limb for a dressier dinner.

Money and timing

  • Prices on islands are often in BZ$; the tip and currency helper converts at the fixed 2:1 rate.
  • Dinner in high season fills up, so book the top picks ahead.
  • Lobster is in season from July to February only.
