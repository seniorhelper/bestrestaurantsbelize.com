About Best Restaurants Belize

Who we are, how the site is funded, and how we check facts.

Best Restaurants Belize answers one question: where should I eat in Belize? It is a town-by-town directory of named restaurants, each with links to the public sources behind it and the month we last checked it.

The site is an independent project of Zach Wennstedt, published by Eye To Ad Media in Denver, Colorado. No restaurant, resort or tour operator owns it or approves what we write.

We do not show star scores, review counts or "best of" badges we did not earn ourselves. If a business ever pays for visibility here, its placement carries a Sponsored label and a sponsored link attribute. Our sister site, Belize Restaurants Guide, covers dishes, seasons and food culture.
