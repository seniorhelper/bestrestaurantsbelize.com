Where to eat in Placencia

A long, narrow peninsula with a village at its tip and beach resorts along the way. Small, but one of Belize’s best places to eat.

The Placencia peninsula stretches roughly sixteen miles from Riversdale down to Placencia Village, with the Caribbean on one side and a lagoon on the other. Maya Beach and Seine Bight sit along the way. Most restaurants cluster in the village around the main road and the famous pedestrian sidewalk; a few of the best are up the peninsula at the resorts.

In the village

  • Rumfish y Vino is the dinner reservation to make: Belizean and international fusion and a good wine list on an open-air upstairs patio.
  • Barefoot Bar is the beach bar, famous for its catch-of-the-day lobster grilled cheese, salbutes and cheap drink specials.
  • Wendy’s Creole Restaurant (not the chain) near the pier serves Belizean, Mexican and international food and is a reliable breakfast stop.
  • Rick’s Cafe sits upstairs above Tutti Frutti Gelateria; dinner up, gelato down.
  • The Secret Garden is a small, candlelit option tucked away in the village.

Up the peninsula

The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel, around mile 15, serves a French spin on Belizean food all day on the beach. AFAR says it has twice been Belize’s restaurant of the year; recent reviews are mixed, so read the latest. Combine it with a beach afternoon at Maya Beach.

Tips

  • Taxis and golf carts link the village and Maya Beach; arrange a ride back after dinner.
  • Book Rumfish and the Bistro on weekends and in high season.
  • Gelato at Tutti Frutti is the evening ritual; check its closing day before you go.
