Where to eat breakfast in Belize

Belizean breakfasts are big, early and built around fry jacks. Here is where to have them.

Breakfast matters in Belize because days start early: dive boats leave at dawn, tours head for the ruins before the heat, and fishermen are back by mid-morning. The classic plate is fry jacks (puffed fried dough) with refried beans, eggs and cheese, sometimes with bacon or ham and always with strong coffee or fresh juice. Our sister site explains fry jacks and johnny cakes.

By town

  • Caye Caulker: Errolyn’s House of Fry Jacks (go early, there is a queue) and Ice & Beans for coffee and mini doughnuts on the beach.
  • San Pedro: Estel’s Dine by the Sea, tables in the sand. Blue Water Grill and Palmilla also serve breakfast.
  • Placencia: Wendy’s, a local sit-down favorite near the pier. Up the peninsula, The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel serves all day.
  • San Ignacio: Pop’s Restaurant, which Frommer’s calls the most popular breakfast spot in the area, and Running W for a resort breakfast.

Tips

  • Breakfast places fill before tour departures; arrive early or late.
  • Ask for "fry jacks with beans and egg" for the full local version.
  • Belizean habanero sauce is on the table; go easy.
