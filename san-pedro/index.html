Where to eat

Where to eat in San Pedro and on Ambergris Caye

Belize’s busiest food scene, from Front Street icons to beach bars north of the bridge. Organized by where you are staying.

San Pedro is the only town on Ambergris Caye, and most restaurants sit along its three parallel streets (Front, Middle and Back) or along the beach. North of the bridge, a road and a string of resorts run up the island; south of town, the island narrows past the airstrip toward more resorts. Golf carts and water taxis connect it all.

In town

The classics are here. Elvi’s Kitchen, open since 1974, is the sand-floored icon. El Fogon cooks Belizean food on a wood-fire hearth and sells out at lunch. Caramba lets you choose your fish from the display. Blue Water Grill is an oceanfront all-day institution with sushi nights. For breakfast with your feet in the sand, Estel’s Dine by the Sea. Hidden Treasure remains the romantic dinner pick; its 2026 posts announce a move north, so confirm where it is when you book.

South of town

Staying at one of the southern resorts? Palmilla at Victoria House, about two miles south, is open to non-guests for breakfast, lunch and dinner with ocean views. For the opposite end of the scale, Robin’s Kitchen is a roadside jerk-chicken stand that draws a crowd; hours can be limited.

North of the bridge

The Truck Stop, about a mile north of the bridge, is Belize’s first shipping-container food park, with several vendors, a bar and a pool. Further up, Rojo Beach Bar & Lounge beside Azul Resort is a stylish beach bar for creative plates and sunset drinks; Fodor’s notes it closes Sundays and Mondays. Getting this far north usually means a water taxi or a long cart ride, so plan your return.

Tips for eating on Ambergris Caye

  • Reserve dinner in high season (December to April) at Elvi’s, Hidden Treasure and Palmilla.
  • Ask about card surcharges; small places may prefer cash. Prices are often in BZ$; divide by two for US$. Try our helper.
  • Seafood seasons: lobster from July 1 to the end of February, conch from October 1 until the quota closes it.

Restaurants in San Pedro

Belizean · Seafood

Elvi's Kitchen

San Pedro town

San Pedro’s best-known restaurant. It began in 1974 when Elvi Staines sold burgers from her window, then grew around a tree that still stands in the sand-floored dining room. Traditional Belizean plates and fresh seafood; reservations help in high season.

Questions

Which part of Ambergris Caye is best for restaurants?

Town has the most choice within walking distance. North and south of town you rely on your resort, a few standout restaurants and carts or water taxis.

Is Elvi’s Kitchen worth it?

It is the island’s most famous restaurant, with a 50-year story and a sand floor. Go for the setting and classic Belizean plates; book in high season.
