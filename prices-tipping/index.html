Money tool

Belize restaurant prices, tipping and BZ$ helper

Read a Belize menu in two currencies, see whether tax and service are included, and split the bill.

The BZ$ to US$ conversion uses the fixed peg of 2:1. Card terminals and exchange houses may apply their own fees.

Two currencies, one easy rule

The Belize dollar has been pegged to the US dollar at 2 to 1 for decades, maintained by the Central Bank of Belize. Menus on the islands may be in either currency, so check which before you order: a "35" lobster dinner is very different in BZ$ and US$. Divide BZ$ by two for US$, or double US$ for BZ$.

What gets added

Belize’s General Sales Tax is 12.5% on most goods and services, and a 2026 cost-of-living report, citing the government agency BELTRAIDE, says the 2026/27 budget left it unchanged. Some restaurants include it in menu prices, others add it at the end, and some add a service charge. The calculator lets you match whatever your bill shows.

Tipping

When no service charge is included, a tip of around 10–15% is common at sit-down restaurants; at counters and fry-jack stands, rounding up is appreciated. If the bill already shows service, any extra is up to you.

What meals cost, roughly

We do not publish restaurant prices because they change and vary by season. As a rule of thumb, local diners, fry-jack counters and street food are the cheapest meals, beach bars and casual grills sit in the middle, and resort and fine-dining restaurants cost the most, with lobster the priciest item on most menus. Islands are generally more expensive than the mainland because much food is shipped in.

Money questions

Can I pay in US dollars in Belize?

Yes. US dollars are widely accepted and the Belize dollar is pegged at BZ$2 to US$1, a rate the Central Bank of Belize has held for decades. Change is often given in Belize dollars.

Is tax included in restaurant prices?

Belize charges a 12.5% General Sales Tax on most goods and services. Some menus include it and others add it, and some restaurants add a service charge. Read the bill.

How much should I tip in Belize?

If no service charge is on the bill, many visitors tip around 10–15% at sit-down restaurants. If service is already included, extra is optional.
