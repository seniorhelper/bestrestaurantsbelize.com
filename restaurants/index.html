Belizean · Seafood

Elvi's Kitchen

San Pedro town

San Pedro’s best-known restaurant. It began in 1974 when Elvi Staines sold burgers from her window, then grew around a tree that still stands in the sand-floored dining room. Traditional Belizean plates and fresh seafood; reservations help in high season.

About locations

Belize addresses are often informal ("north of the bridge", "near the pier"). We give the area and any address the business or a guidebook publishes, and every card links to an OpenStreetMap search so you can get directions. We do not pin restaurants on a map until we can verify the exact spot.

How we choose

Places are listed when public sources such as guidebook publishers (Fodor’s, Frommer’s), travel magazines, local guides, the business itself and major review platforms confirm them. Descriptions are written by us. Closed places are removed and logged on our editorial policy page.
