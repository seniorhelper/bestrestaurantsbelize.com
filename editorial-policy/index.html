Editorial

Editorial policy and change log

Our standards, and what we corrected or removed.

How we build and maintain the directory.

Criteria for a listing

  • Confirmation in public sources: a guidebook publisher such as Fodor's or Frommer's, a travel magazine, a local guide, the business's own site or social account, or a large review platform. Thinly sourced entries are flagged in our data file.
  • Each card lists its sources and the month we checked.
  • Real cuisine labels, so a gelateria is a gelateria, not "Belizean".

Things we refuse to publish

  • Ratings or review counts we did not gather.
  • Opening hours or prices unless the business publishes them.
  • Composite or imaginary "venues" standing in for a food tradition.

Tags

Occasion tags in the finder (romantic, where locals eat, water view) are the editor's reading of the cited sources, never a paid feature.

Locations

Belize addresses are informal, so we name the area and link to an OpenStreetMap search. We will pin exact locations only after verifying them.

Review cycle

We re-check every listing at least once a quarter and record removals below.

Change log

  • Fuego Bar & Grill (San Ignacio): Removed: a directory lists it as permanently closed (checked October 2026).
  • "Garifuna Kitchens (Hopkins)" and "Belize City Creole Tables": Removed from the restaurant list and structured data: these described food traditions, not real businesses. Hopkins now lists named Garifuna restaurants.
  • servesCuisine: "Belizean" on every listing: Corrected. Each restaurant now shows its real cuisines (for example gelato, French-inspired, Jamaican).
  • Lobster season dates: Corrected to July 1 to the end of February (closed March 1 to June 30) under Statutory Instrument 128 of 2021, as announced by the Fisheries Department.
