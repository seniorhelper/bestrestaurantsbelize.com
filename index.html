Short answer

San Pedro on Ambergris Caye has the most restaurants; Placencia and San Ignacio have the strongest dinner scenes for their size. Start with Elvi’s Kitchen and El Fogon in San Pedro, Errolyn’s fry jacks on Caye Caulker, Rumfish y Vino and The Bistro at Maya Beach in Placencia, Innie’s for Garifuna food in Hopkins, and Guava Limb in San Ignacio. Book dinner ahead from December to April.

Belize is a small country with a famously big table. Squeezed between Mexico and Guatemala, with a Caribbean coast and a jungle interior, it cooks with Creole, Garifuna, Mestizo, Maya, Chinese and East Indian influences. This site is about where to eat: named restaurants in each town, with the sources we used and the month we checked them. For what to eat, from hudut to fry jacks, our sister site Belize Restaurants Guide explains the dishes and food culture.

The legends

The restaurants that built Belize’s reputation

A handful of places are more than restaurants; they are institutions. Here is why they matter.

Read the stories or jump to the fine-dining guide.

Elvi’s Kitchen: San Pedro’s heart since 1974

No restaurant tells the story of eating on Ambergris Caye better. Elvi Staines started by selling burgers from the window of her house in 1974, then put a few tables on the sand under a tree. The floor is still sand and the tree still grows through the dining room. Today the menu runs to whole fried fish, seafood and traditional rice and beans with stew chicken. Book in high season.

El Fogon: cooking the old way

If Elvi’s is the grand institution, El Fogon is its soulful counterpart. It is named for the open wood-fire hearth, and it cooks Belizean home food the traditional way in a simple palapa. Guides call it a locals’ lunch legend that sells out, so arrive early.

Hidden Treasure: fine dining, island style

For years Hidden Treasure has been the island’s romantic standard, glowing with lamplight and known for barbecue ribs seasoned with Garifuna spices. In 2026 its posts announced a new location north of town, so check the address when you reserve.

The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel: Placencia’s award magnet

Up the Placencia peninsula at Maya Beach, this beachfront bistro puts a French spin on Belizean food, and AFAR reports it has twice been named Belize’s restaurant of the year. Some 2026 reviews are critical, so read recent ones, but the setting on the sand is as good as ever.

Guava Limb and Running W: the jungle’s dinner stars

Inland, San Ignacio has quietly become one of the country’s best places to eat. Guava Limb Cafe serves candlelit dinners in a garden near the Macal River, and Running W at the San Ignacio Resort Hotel offers refined cooking in a jungle setting from breakfast to dinner.

From beach shack to white tablecloth

The remarkable thing about eating in Belize is the range. In a single day on the Placencia peninsula you can have a barefoot breakfast under a thatched roof and dinner at a beachfront bistro up the road. San Pedro packs Caribbean, Mexican, Italian, Asian and American kitchens into a few walkable blocks, with the priciest waterfront tables and cheap back-street spots a couple of minutes apart. San Ignacio has become an inland dining hub. And everywhere, the family-run kitchen, the fry-jack counter and the grill where you choose your fish remain the soul of the scene.

Use this directory as your backbone, then ask locals where they ate last night. Some of the best meals in Belize cost a few dollars and come with sand between your toes.

Best restaurants in Belize: FAQ

What is the best restaurant in Belize?

There is no single answer; it depends on where you are and what you want. Elvi’s Kitchen in San Pedro is the classic, open since 1974. For a special night, look at Hidden Treasure or Palmilla on Ambergris Caye, The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel or Rumfish y Vino in Placencia, and Guava Limb or Running W in San Ignacio. For local flavor, El Fogon’s hearth cooking and Innie’s Garifuna food in Hopkins are hard to beat.

Which Belize town has the best restaurants?

San Pedro on Ambergris Caye has the most choice by far. Placencia has an outsized dining scene for its size, San Ignacio is the inland food hub, Caye Caulker does barefoot seafood and breakfast, and Hopkins is where to find Garifuna cooking.

What is the most famous restaurant in San Pedro?

Elvi’s Kitchen. Elvi Staines began by selling burgers from her window in 1974, and the sand floor and the tree in the dining room are still there. It serves Belizean plates and seafood.

Where can I find fine dining in Belize?

On Ambergris Caye: Hidden Treasure and Palmilla at Victoria House. In Placencia: The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel and Rumfish y Vino. In Cayo: Guava Limb and Running W. Even the dressiest places are island-casual. See our fine-dining guide.

Where should I eat in Placencia?

Rumfish y Vino for dinner, Barefoot Bar for a lobster grilled cheese on the sand, Wendy’s for a local sit-down meal, Rick’s Cafe upstairs and Tutti Frutti gelato downstairs, and The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel up the peninsula. Details on the Placencia page.

Where should I eat in San Ignacio?

Guava Limb Cafe and Running W for dinner, Pop’s for breakfast, and Cenaida’s for home-style Belizean plates. See the San Ignacio page.

Where can I eat Garifuna food?

In Hopkins, Innie’s Restaurant and Tugusina Garifuna serve hudut and other Garifuna dishes. Order hudut ahead if you can, because it takes time to make.

Do I need reservations at Belize restaurants?

For dinner at popular spots in the December to April high season, yes. Book Hidden Treasure, Palmilla, Rumfish y Vino, Guava Limb and The Bistro at Maya Beach ahead. Beach bars and breakfast spots are walk-in.

Can I pay by card at Belize restaurants?

Larger restaurants and resorts generally take cards, sometimes with a surcharge; small island spots may be cash only. Belize dollars and US dollars are both used at a fixed rate of 2 to 1. Our tip and currency helper does the math.

When can I order lobster in Belize?

Lobster season runs from July 1 to the end of February; it is closed from March 1 to June 30. Conch opens October 1 and runs until June 30 or until the national quota is reached. Restaurants should not serve either out of season.
