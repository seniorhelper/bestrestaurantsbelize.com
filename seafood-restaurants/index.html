Where to eat seafood in Belize

The reef is the country’s pantry. Here is where to eat its catch, and when lobster and conch are legal.

With the Belize Barrier Reef offshore, seafood is the backbone of coastal menus: snapper, grouper, shrimp, and in season, spiny lobster and queen conch. Both lobster and conch are regulated by the Belize Fisheries Department, so what you can order depends on the month.

Seasons for 2026–27

  • Lobster: open July 1 to the end of February; closed March 1 to June 30 (Statutory Instrument 128 of 2021). The 2026–27 season opened on July 1, 2026, and runs to February 28, 2027.
  • Conch: opens October 1 and runs to June 30 or until the national quota is reached. The 2025–26 season closed more than two months early when the quota was met, and it is expected to reopen on October 1, 2026.

If either appears on a menu out of season, ask where it came from, or order snapper, grouper or shrimp instead. Our sister site keeps a month-by-month seafood calendar.

Where to go

  • Pick your catch: Caramba in San Pedro.
  • Seafood with a view: Blue Water Grill and Palmilla on Ambergris Caye, Rainbow Grill over the water on Caye Caulker.
  • Beach-bar classics: Barefoot Bar’s lobster grilled cheese in Placencia; seasonal seafood at The Lazy Lizard.
  • Market-driven dinners: Chef Rob’s in Hopkins and Elvi’s Kitchen in San Pedro.
  • Garifuna seafood: hudut at Innie’s in Hopkins.

Belizean · Seafood

Elvi's Kitchen

San Pedro town

San Pedro’s best-known restaurant. It began in 1974 when Elvi Staines sold burgers from her window, then grew around a tree that still stands in the sand-floored dining room. Traditional Belizean plates and fresh seafood; reservations help in high season.
