Where to eat on Caye Caulker

A small, car-free island where the motto is "go slow". Here is where to eat from dawn to the last drink at The Split.

Caye Caulker is a short water-taxi ride south of Ambergris Caye and much smaller: sandy streets, golf carts and bicycles, and a waterfront lined with docks. The island’s food is casual and fresh. Breakfast is a serious event, lunch is often grilled fish, and evenings center on The Split, the channel at the north end of the village.

A day of eating

  1. Breakfast: queue at Errolyn’s House of Fry Jacks for the island’s signature stuffed fry jacks, or take coffee and warm mini doughnuts at Ice & Beans on the beachfront (vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, according to one guide).
  2. Lunch: grilled seafood over the water at Rainbow Grill & Bar.
  3. Afternoon: swim and a drink at The Lazy Lizard at The Split.
  4. Dinner: book Hibisca by Habanero for a dressier meal, or eat barbecue and grilled fish in Wish Willy’s backyard.

Island tips

  • Many places are cash-friendly rather than card-friendly; bring small notes in BZ$ or US$.
  • Service is relaxed. Order, settle in and enjoy it.
  • Lobster season is July to February; in July, check local news for island festivities when the season opens.
  • Learn the dishes before you go on our sister site’s street food guide.
