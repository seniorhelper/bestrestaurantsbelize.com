Where to eat

Fine dining and date nights in Belize

Where to book when the occasion matters, from candlelit Ambergris Caye to the Cayo jungle.

Belize does special-occasion dining its own way: the cooking can be refined, but the mood stays relaxed. Jackets are almost never needed, and many of the best tables are outdoors, so think sea breeze rather than chandeliers.

Ambergris Caye

Hidden Treasure is the island’s romantic classic, known for Garifuna-spiced ribs; confirm its 2026 location. Palmilla at Victoria House is resort dining with ocean views, about two miles south of town. Rojo Beach Bar up north suits a stylish sunset drink and small plates.

Caye Caulker

Hibisca by Habanero is the island’s dressier dinner.

Placencia peninsula

Rumfish y Vino for fusion and wine on an open-air patio; The Bistro at Maya Beach Hotel for a beach setting and a much-awarded past; The Secret Garden for candlelight in the village.

Hopkins

Chef Rob’s builds a daily menu from the market, with lobster and conch in season.

Cayo

Guava Limb Cafe serves candlelit dinners in a garden; Running W offers refined cooking at the San Ignacio Resort Hotel.

Booking tips

  • Reserve a few days ahead from December to April.
  • Ask whether service is included and whether cards carry a surcharge.
  • For lobster, come between July and February.
